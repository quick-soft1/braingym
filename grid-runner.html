📖 How to Play

🎯 Objective: Complete all 30 rounds! Watch the flashing grid sequence for each round and tap the identical path from memory.

🔥 30-Level Grid Complexity:

  • Rounds 1–10 (4x4 Grid): 3 to 12 steps. Standard flash timing.
  • Rounds 11–20 (5x5 Grid): 13 to 22 steps. Faster playback speeds!
  • Rounds 21–30 (6x6 Grid): 23 to 32 steps on a massive 36-tile grid!

⚙️ Game Buttons:

  • 🔄 Replay: Re-watch the current path sequence without losing a life.
  • 🎨 Theme: Customize game colors.
  • 📖 Rules: Open instructions and level breakdown.
  • 🔊 Sound: Toggle audio effects.

❤️ Lives: You have 3 lives. Tapping an incorrect tile deducts 1 life and replays the current sequence.
